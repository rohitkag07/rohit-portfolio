## Resume Tips:

- Keep it to 1-2 pages
- Use a clean, professional template
- Include: Contact info, Summary, Skills, Experience, Education, Projects
- Quantify achievements (numbers, percentages, impact)
- Tailor it to the job you're applying for
- Proofread carefully (no typos!)

## ATS-Friendly Format:

- Use standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman)
- Avoid images, graphics, tables
- Use standard section headings
- Include keywords from job descriptions
- Save as PDF (not Word doc)

When the resume changes, replace `public/rohit-kag-it-resume.pdf` first and keep the legacy copies in sync if old links need to continue working.
